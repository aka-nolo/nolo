      Before we talk

      What you might want to know first.

      What can I hire you for?

      Four things: architectural design and BIM (Revit modeling, documentation and renders), websites, brands and internal apps, and video — from concept and direction to the final cut. Clients usually come for one and stay for another.

      Do you build software for architecture and construction?

      Yes. I write Revit plugins in C# (.NET, Revit 2023–2027), automate BIM workflows with Dynamo and Python, and build Power Apps and AI agents for AEC teams. Because I'm an architect, you don't have to translate your model, your standards or your deadlines.

      Where are you, and how do we work together?

      I work remotely in English and Spanish, with teams in Norway, Iceland and Latin America. Clear scope up front, a working draft early, and documentation at handover.

      How do we start?

      Book a short call or write to me on WhatsApp. Tell me what you're building and when you need it — I'll come back with a scope and a plan.
